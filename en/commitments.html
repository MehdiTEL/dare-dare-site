Commitments

Agence Dare-Dare's commitments: Our
commitments.

What we promise our clients, their guests and everyone who works with us. Nothing more than what we actually do.

01

Events that leave a mark. Not on the planet.

Our CSR charter, applied to every project and offered to every client:

  • Working local: suppliers, craftspeople and producers close to the venue; in Morocco, a selected local network paid fairly
  • Fighting waste: quantities matched to the real guest count, seasonal menus, vegetarian options, surplus food redistributed when possible
  • Reuse rather than throw away: hired and reused decor and furniture, demountable scenography, fewer single-use prints and goodies
  • Lower-impact travel: train for journeys within France, shared transfers, venues reachable by public transport
  • Sorting and managing waste: sorting with venues and caterers, reusable tableware, as little single-use plastic as possible
  • At least one more responsible alternative in every proposal

Read our full CSR charter

02

Welcoming everyone

  • Accessibility of venues for people with reduced mobility
  • Dietary needs and specific requirements of every guest
  • Diversity of the talent, artists and speakers we book
  • An alcohol-free menu at every event; alcohol is never the hero
03

Caring for the teams

  • Realistic schedules, with breaks and meals for everyone on site
  • Partners and freelancers paid on the agreed terms
  • Safety on every set-up and load-out
04

Clear and honest

  • Detailed line-by-line quotes, fees stated separately
  • Straight talk about what is feasible on a given budget
  • Covered by professional liability insurance: 2026 certificate available on request
  • Our work: projects delivered in-house and as freelance, presented as such
  • Real photos only: our events, or credited inspiration images, never an AI-generated image presented as our work
05

Giving and passing it on

We aim to give 1 to 2% of our profits each year to the Association Petits Princes, which makes the dreams of seriously ill children and teenagers come true.

Every year we welcome interns, involved in real projects from brief to event day, to open event careers to a wider range of people.
