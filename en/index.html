The Daring Agency

From Fnac Live to private Longchamp events, through the Moulin Rouge, Dare-Dare orchestrates parties where standards fade behind the experience. Public or ultra-VIP, every format is mastered.

Dare-Dare is the agency that turns every brief into a memorable moment. Samsung, Chanel, Dior, Bottega Veneta, Hermès, Longchamp, Mugler: prestigious houses that trust us to lead their most ambitious event projects, from 1,500-person concerts to VIP dinners beneath the Louvre Pyramid.

The agency stands for one belief: You dare us, we did it.

Today, our team operates between Paris, Cannes and Morocco, delivering exceptional events across both sides of the Mediterranean.

And because an event can do good beyond the party: our ambition is to donate 1 to 2% of our profits every year to the Association Petits Princes, which makes the dreams of seriously ill children come true. Every year, we also welcome interns to pass on our know-how. Read our CSR charter
