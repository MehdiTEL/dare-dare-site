What can an event agency organise around the 2030 World Cup in Morocco?

Morocco will host the 2030 World Cup with Spain and Portugal (appointed on 11 December 2024). Agence Dare-Dare, which is its own DMC in Morocco, produces the events of brands and companies around the tournament: private match-night parties, client hosting, retreats and incentives, product launches, press and influencer trips, with logistics on the ground in Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Fez and Agadir. We do not sell tickets or hospitality packages: we create what happens around the matches.

What is known,
what is not yet.

Confirmed

  • On 11 December 2024, an Extraordinary FIFA Congress appointed Morocco, Spain and Portugal for the 2030 World Cup.
  • Three centenary matches will be played in Argentina, Paraguay and Uruguay.
  • The Moroccan bid presented six cities: Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Fez and Agadir.

Not yet published

  • Match dates and the calendar.
  • The split of matches between Moroccan cities.
  • We announce nothing that the organiser has not published.

Our position

  • No link with the organisers or their brands.
  • Events described by their venue and format, not by distinctive signs.
  • Options placed early, confirmed when the calendar is.

Sources: FIFA, "Hosts appointed for 2030 and 2034 FIFA World Cups" (fifa.com) and the Extraordinary Congress release of 11 December 2024 (inside.fifa.com); bid cities: Moroccan press (TelQuel, 2 August 2024), to be confirmed with the organiser.
