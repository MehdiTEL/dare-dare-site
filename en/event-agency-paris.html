What we handle in the shadows

Venues and access

  • Seated, cocktail and theatre capacities; opening hours and sound limits
  • Set-up and dismantling (the day before? overnight?), delivery access, goods lifts
  • Compulsory vendors, cloakroom, electricity, the right to take photos and video

Permits

  • Music: SACEM declaration, checked with the venue and the DJ
  • Bar or alcohol outside a licensed venue: temporary town-hall permit
  • Public space and filming in the street: permit from the City of Paris (Mission Cinéma)

Safety

  • Maximum capacity of the venue open to the public (ERP)
  • Security staff and first-aiders depending on size
  • A weather plan B for rooftops and terraces

The capacities below come from our guides; they are reconfirmed with each venue and for each date.

Iconic venues, with their capacities

Museums and monuments

  • Louvre Pyramid: 300 to 800 guests; access through the Carrousel, installation very tightly supervised.
  • Carrousel du Louvre: modular spaces for very large formats.
  • Musée d'Orsay: a 138 m nave, up to 1,500 guests for a cocktail; acoustics to be handled.
  • Eiffel Tower, private lounges: 50 to 300 guests; safety files required, drones banned.
  • Palais Brongniart: 200 to 2,000 guests.

Hotels, venues and foundations

  • The Ritz Paris: 30 to 150 guests, the opposite of volume.
  • Moulin Rouge: 200 to 800 guests, privatised: show, dinner, backstage access.
  • Piscine Molitor: 100 to 400 guests; the pool acoustics and the guest flows through the spa need to be known.
  • Fondation Louis Vuitton: 200 to 600 guests, 350-seat auditorium.
  • Hôtel de Ville: the forecourt for the general public, the state rooms inside.

Source: 10 exceptional event venues in Paris, a guide by Agence Dare-Dare.

Best season from May to September; we never book a roof without a plan B.

The method

One venue, one concept,
one team.

01

The brief

We listen: ambition, guest count, date, audience, constraints, budget. We restate it in your words before looking for a venue.

02

The proposal

Two or three venues, a concept, a scenography, an itemised budget. Tailor-made, never copy-paste.

03

Production

Vendors, show running, a schedule counted back from the day. You approve, we execute.

04

The day and the debrief

We stay in the shadows so you step into the spotlight, then we debrief with figures: attendance, press coverage, photos and videos.
