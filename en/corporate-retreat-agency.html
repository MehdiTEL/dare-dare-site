The detail, item by item

Travel and accommodation

  • Hotel: room allotment, release date (after it, unconfirmed rooms are handed back), attrition clause, minimum catering spend
  • Train rather than plane when possible

Programme

  • A balance of work and emotion: not too many plenaries, not too many parties
  • Dietary requirements and step-free access taken into account
  • At least one responsible alternative offered to every client

Teams and the day

  • Final rooming list and last changes handled with the hotel
  • Team confirmed the day before, run of show to the minute
  • A debrief with figures: attendance, satisfaction, impact

The method

A dedicated project manager,
from brief to last departure.

01

The brief

We listen: goals of the retreat, number of participants, dates, profile of the group, constraints (diets, mobility) and budget.

02

The proposal

Two or three destinations or venues, a day-by-day programme, an itemised budget. The site visit is included in the quote.

03

Production

Flights, hotels, transfers, local vendors, negotiated hotel contracts, a run of show to the minute. You approve, we execute.

04

The day and the debrief

We welcome the group and stay until the last departure, in the background but at your side. Then a debrief with figures.
