One format,
according to the angle and the journalists we want to reach.

Press conference

An announcement, short speeches, questions, a time to talk at the end. For an appointment, a partnership, a result or an opening.

Press day

A day of meetings between journalists and spokespeople, in a setting that tells the brand story. To present a collection, a range or a product to the fashion, beauty and lifestyle press.

Press breakfast

A short, friendly, more intimate format, alcohol-free, that leaves room for conversation. For news, a trend, a back-to-work update.

Preview

The press discovers first, before the public: a launch, an exhibition, a venue, an experience. Content creators can be invited alongside the press.

Press trip

Several days, one destination, one story. Our dedicated page details the method.
