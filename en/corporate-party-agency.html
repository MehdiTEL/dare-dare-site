The detail, item by item

Table and bar

  • Standing dinner: 20 to 22 pieces per guest; welcome: a glass and two bites
  • Quantities confirmed as late as possible to limit waste

Teams

  • Hosts picked from a book, outfit, a 20 to 30 second welcome script
  • Stock and bar lead, runner, artist lead
  • Each team confirmed the day before

Permits and safety

  • SACEM: we check who declares (venue, DJ or agency)
  • Bar or alcohol outside a licensed venue: temporary town-hall permit
  • Maximum capacity, security staff, first-aiders depending on size

Three formats
that really bring people together.

A real team party, where everyone joins in and nobody checks their watch. On quote.

01

The New Year Brunch

January, a Friday lunchtime or a Sunday morning: a big brunch in a venue full of light, the management’s greetings in three minutes, then tables that mix the departments. Everyone leaves with the year under way.

02

The Jazz Club

A hushed private salon, a live trio, low tables and a dinner to share. The teams meet as they would in a Parisian club, with no stage and no speech that drags on.

03

The Rooftop Chalet

A Paris rooftop dressed as a winter chalet: wood, blankets, fondue, hot chocolate and a view over the roofs. The December party people are still talking about in January.
