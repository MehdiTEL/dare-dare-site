The venues of Cannes and the coast

Palais des Festivals and the Croisette

  • Palais des Festivals: conventions, tech launches, award ceremonies; 300 to 3,000 guests, 35,000 m² of modular space.
  • Private beaches on the Croisette, five minutes on foot: plenary in the morning, lunch with your feet in the sand, an evening under the stars.

Private beaches

  • Plage Bijou: 500 guests, privatised from 7.30 pm to 2 am, security and lifeguard included.
  • Vilebrequin La Plage: up to 500 guests for a cocktail.
  • Other addresses we looked at: Playa Chica, White House.

Villas

  • Villa Keys: 250 guests for a cocktail, rental only without technical set-up, the venue’s partner caterers.
  • The hills: infinity pools, hanging terraces, 180° panoramas over the bay and the Lérins islands.
  • Demanding logistics: winding access, caterer capacity to plan ahead, precise set-up.

Yachts and the sea

  • From 20 to 150 guests depending on the boat.
  • Sunset cocktail, dinner at anchor off the Lérins islands, after-party on the upper deck.
  • Everything is decided beforehand: tender shuttle, marine weather, real capacity, caterer on board, a plan B on land.

And beyond

The method

A site visit, a plan B,
a dedicated project manager.

01

The brief

We listen: dates (Festival, trade shows or off-season), guest count, mood, constraints (confidentiality, accessibility, alcohol-free version). We check your date against the trade-show calendar before we talk venue.

02

The proposal

Two or three venues (villa, beach, boat, Palais), a concept, an itemised budget. The site visit is included in the quote.

03

Production

Partners on the coast, caterer, technical, security, permits. A run of show to the minute, a weather plan B, one WhatsApp group for all teams.

04

The day and the debrief

Our team is on site from set-up to dismantling, in the background but at your side. Then a debrief with figures: attendance, impact, photos and videos.
