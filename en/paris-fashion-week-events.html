What does an event agency do during Paris Fashion Week?

During show weeks, an event agency produces everything that happens around the shows: brand parties, presentations and showrooms, dinners, VIP hospitality and designer hosting. Agence Dare-Dare designs and produces them in Paris, with a dedicated project lead and the logistics these weeks demand: venues, access, transfers, guest welcome. Our team produced for Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Mugler, Bottega Veneta and Longchamp.

Our calendar advice

A brand party is placed around the shows, not against them: the evening before opening, the night of a much-awaited show, or just after the closing. We cross-check the date with the show schedule, the week's other parties and venue availability before proposing it. And the week is prepared early: venues, caterers and drivers are booked well before the first invitation.

What we always plan

  • Guest list, seating and named welcome
  • Access, transfers and a traffic plan around the shows
  • A written plan B, an alcohol-free menu, diets and allergies

How we produce

Four steps,
a week without a misstep.

01

The brief

Your house, the collection, the audience to win over, the target week, confidentiality constraints. We restate it in your words.

02

The proposal

A concept, two or three venues, an hour-by-hour running order set within the show schedule, a line-by-line budget.

03

Production

Venue, set, catering, technical, welcome, transfers, a written plan B. You approve, we execute.

04

The day and the debrief

A dedicated project lead on site, briefed crews, then a debrief: attendance, photos, feedback from your guests.

Allow 3 to 4 months before the show week: venues, caterers, drivers and crews are booked early, and the best addresses go fast. A shorter timeline is possible, with fewer venues to choose from.
A presentation shows the collection to a chosen audience (press, buyers, clients) in a short, guided format. A showroom receives by appointment, over one or several days. A brand party gathers a wider crowd around one big moment. We produce all three, sometimes in the same venue at different times.
The show schedule is managed by the Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode, where fashion houses register, and Agence Dare-Dare is not affiliated with the Fédération. We produce the events around the shows. For a collection presentation, we design the format with you.
Looking after key guests (clients, press, talent): guest list and seating, transfers, a private lounge or reserved table, cloakroom, timings set around the shows and a dedicated contact on site. The level of service is agreed at the brief.
We do not publish standard prices: the budget depends on the venue, the guest count, the format and the production. We reply within 24 hours with an itemised, line-by-line quote.
The bar is a service, never the subject of the evening: water and soft drinks everywhere, an alcohol-free menu as carefully made as the rest, quantities worked out. If the event features an alcohol brand, French law (loi Évin) restricts its communication and we check it before any invitation goes out.
