The short answer

Agence Dare-Dare designs and produces the scenography of luxury events in Paris: a concept, set design, lighting, furniture, signage and the guest journey, then fabrication and installation on site. Every detail follows one idea, from invitation to dismantling. Our team produced scenography for Dior, Chanel, Longchamp, Moët & Chandon and Deloitte, at the Louvre, the Ritz Paris and Palais Brongniart.

What our scenography covers

Concept

one simple, strong idea that sets the thread for the set, the objects and the story of the night.

Set design

volumes, materials, flowers and structures drawn for the venue, heavy or light depending on the project.

Lighting

lighting plan, projections, architecture and products brought out, designed for photo and video too.

Furniture and table

rental, sourcing or bespoke fabrication: seating, bars, set tables, tableware, linen, flowers.

Signage

welcome, cloakroom, bar, exits: signage drawn with the art direction, in the brand's tone.

Guest journey

from arrival to exit: zones, key moments, flows, how many people at once.

Fabrication and installation

workshops, deliveries, night installation, safety checks, then dismantling and what becomes of the set.

Our method, from brief to dismantling

01 The brief

we listen: ambition, brand, guest count, date, constraints, budget.

02 The concept

one thread, a plan per zone, a lighting direction, materials, an itemised budget.

03 Fabrication

workshops, rental, sourcing, flowers, signage, a schedule counted back from the day.

04 Installation and the day

deliveries, installation, lighting settings, a run-through of the journey.

05 Dismantling and debrief

dismantling within venue hours, reuse or recycling of the set, then a debrief with figures, photos and video.
