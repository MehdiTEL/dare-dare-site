The detail, item by item

Before

  • Brief: product, target, message, on-sale date, embargoes
  • Concept, two or three venues, an itemised budget
  • Invitations overbooked by 20 to 30%, RSVP follow-ups

On the day

  • Set-up the day before, team briefing beforehand
  • Welcome, bar, reveal, content, guest management
  • One WhatsApp group for all teams

Afterwards

  • Dismantling, inventory, venue restored
  • A debrief with figures: attendance, satisfaction, press and social impact
  • Photos and videos delivered

Legal framework

  • Music: SACEM declaration
  • Alcohol: the Évin law, communication is regulated
  • Filming in the street: permit from the City
  • Image rights: agreement before showing a recognisable guest

The method

One thread,
held from the first teaser to the day after.

01

The brief

We listen: the product, the target, the message, the on-sale date, the constraints (alcohol, confidentiality, embargo).

02

The proposal

A named concept, two or three venues, the scenography, the budget item by item. The thread fits in one sentence.

03

Production

Venue, vendors, technical visit, invitations, final figures. You approve, we execute.

04

The day and the debrief

We stay in the shadows during the reveal, then a debrief with figures: attendance, press and social impact, photos and videos.
