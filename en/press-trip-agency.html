Frequently asked questions
about our press trip agency

A press trip invites journalists and creators to discover a brand, a product or a destination over several days. It is not a flight and a hotel: it is an editorial narrative where every moment aims at one thing, making people want to write.
Eight to fifteen guests beat thirty, with a personal follow-up for each one. We have produced confidential formats with six guests and international roll-outs with thirty.
Allow 2 to 3 months in France and 3 to 4 months abroad. The invitation goes out 6 to 8 weeks before the trip.
A press trip aims at articles and reports, with an editorial brief and embargoes. An influencer trip aims at social content, with a precise legal framework: a disclosed collaboration and a written contract. We mix both according to your goal.
Paris, Cannes and the French Riviera, Marrakech and Morocco (private riads, the Agafay desert, medina craftspeople), Venice, and elsewhere on request.
The budget depends on the destination, the length, the number of guests and the programme. We do not publish standard prices: we reply within 24 hours with an itemised, line-by-line quote.
Embargoes are written into the invitation and the media kit, and respected in the schedule. If the brand is an alcohol brand, the Loi Évin regulates communication: we check it for the invitation and the content.
We deliver a report: press review, audience, repeated messages, content produced and recommendations. A free PDF checklist sums up the 7 steps and 35 checkpoints.
