1. The editorial angle worksheet

A press trip starts with the story you want to see told, not with the venue. Fill in this one-page worksheet, get it signed off internally, then stop changing it: it is the compass of the project.

The angle in one sentence Formula: "This trip shows that [brand] [does / defends / invents] [what], and proves it through [central experience]." Your sentence: ...................................................

The angle test (3 questions: three yeses, or start again)

  • Can a journalist sum it up as an article headline without naming the brand?
  • Is it new for their readers, or already seen a hundred times?
  • Can it be proven on site, with people, places and gestures, and not only with a speech?
