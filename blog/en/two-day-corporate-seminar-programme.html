2-day corporate seminar: the sample programme, in Paris or Morocco

Agence Dare-Dare · October 2026 · 9 min read · Published 26 October 2026

The short answer A 2-day corporate seminar works in two beats: day 1 to work and reconnect (welcome, plenary, workshops, a dinner in a venue that stands out) and day 2 to decide and leave with a direction (synthesis workshop, team activity, closing). In Paris everything happens by day and evening in a private venue, with no overnight stay required; in Morocco, one night on site in Marrakech and an evening in the Agafay desert. Either way: a pace that alternates work and emotion, and few plenaries.

The two-day seminar is the most requested format: long enough for teams to really reconnect, short enough to fit a diary. Yet many leadership teams prepare it as a three-day programme cut in two.

Here are the two programmes we build most often, hour by hour, one in Paris and one in Morocco, with the decisions to take before writing them.

01

Organising a 2-day seminar: five decisions before the programme

Objective Guest count Destination

A two-day seminar is built backwards: decide what the teams should take home, then write the programme. Five decisions first:

  • The objective: decide, align, bring people together, train? It sets the share of work and the share of emotion.
  • The guest count: from a management committee to several hundred people, the format changes.
  • The destination: Paris, with no overnight stay required, or Morocco, with one night on site.
  • The date: to cross-check with school holidays, long weekends and, in Morocco, Ramadan (see our 2027 calendar).
  • The budget, given at the brief: it guides the venue, the travel and the catering.

Our brief template asks these questions in order.

02

The sample programme in Paris

Paris Private venue No overnight

Many Paris seminars run without an overnight stay: teams go home, which lightens the logistics. A hotel night is only planned for colleagues from other regions or abroad. The seminar takes place in a private venue (hotel, mansion, rooftop, museum): see our 10 event venues in Paris.

Day 1: work and reconnect

  • 9:00 am: welcome, coffee, badges.
  • 9:30 am: opening plenary, objectives and direction from leadership.
  • 11:00 am: workshops in sub-groups, mixing departments.
  • 12:45 pm: seated lunch, in another space of the venue.
  • 2:30 pm: second work session, then feedback.
  • 7:00 pm: dinner in a venue different from the working one, with one big moment (a guest, a set, a short show).
  • 10:30 pm: end of the evening and arranged journeys home.

Day 2: decide and leave with a direction

  • 9:30 am: breakfast.
  • 10:00 am: synthesis workshop, decisions and owners.
  • 12:00 pm: lunch.
  • 1:30 pm: team activity, for example a team building treasure hunt in an exceptional venue.
  • 4:00 pm: closing, commitments, a word from leadership.
  • 5:00 pm: departure.

03

The sample programme in Morocco

Marrakech Riad Agafay

In Morocco, the two-day seminar is built around one night on site and an evening in the Agafay desert. The group's flight times decide the running order: we write the programme from the flights, not the other way round.

Day 1: arrive and work

  • Morning: airport welcome, transfers, mint tea at the riad, rooms ready.
  • Lunch: on the terrace of the riad or estate.
  • 3:00 pm: plenary in a medina palace or an estate, with the technical side handled end to end.
  • 5:30 pm: workshops in sub-groups.
  • 8:00 pm: welcome dinner on a medina rooftop.

Day 2: decide, then the desert

  • 9:30 am: synthesis workshop, decisions and owners.
  • 12:30 pm: lunch.
  • 3:00 pm: drive to the Agafay desert, team activity: a hike, an orienteering challenge or cooking over a fire.
  • Evening: dinner in a tent, musicians, campfire; back to Marrakech or departure depending on flights.

04

Paris or Morocco: how to choose

Compare Logistics

Paris suits a team already in the region, a tight travel budget or a diary that leaves only a day and a half. Morocco suits a team you want to pull away from daily routine: the change of scene does half the programme, with no time difference.

Morocco needs more logistics (group flights, transfers, accommodation), and that is our trade: we are our own DMC there. Both formats are prepared with the same method: a dedicated project lead, a day-by-day programme, a line-by-line quote.

05

The mistakes that sink a seminar

Pace Pitfalls

Too many plenaries and people switch off. Too many parties and they forget why they came. Our pacing rules are simple:

  • Alternate a work session and an emotion session, never two plenaries in a row.
  • Plan real free time, however short, in the middle of the first day.
  • Do not end the evening too late when work resumes at 9:30 am.
  • Plan diets, accessibility and an alternative to the main activity.
  • Write a plan B for the weather and for the flights.

Our checklist before you start

  • An objective in one sentence, shared with the whole team.
  • A guest count, a date checked against the calendar and a budget given at the brief.
  • One venue to work in and a different one for dinner.
  • At most one plenary per half-day, workshops in between.
  • Diets, accessibility, a plan B for the weather and for the flights.
  • A dedicated project lead on site from the first welcome to the last departure.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sample programme for a 2-day seminar?

Day 1: welcome, opening plenary, workshops, lunch, feedback and a dinner in a venue that stands out. Day 2: breakfast, synthesis workshop, lunch, team activity, closing and departure. In Morocco, the evening of day 2 is often spent in the Agafay desert.

How far ahead should you organise a 2-day seminar?

Allow 3 to 6 months, more for a large group, and 4 to 6 months for a seminar abroad. A shorter timeline is possible, with fewer venues to choose from.

Do you need an overnight stay for a 2-day seminar?

In Paris, no, except for participants from far away. In Morocco, yes: the seminar is built around one night on site and an evening in the desert.

When is the best time for a 2-day seminar in Morocco?

March to May and September to November, when days are mild. Check the dates of Ramadan, which change every year.

How many participants can a 2-day seminar host?

From a management committee to several hundred people. The format changes with the guest count: the larger the group, the more preparation transfers, rooms and catering need.

How much does a 2-day seminar cost?

The budget depends on the destination, the venue, the number of participants and the programme. We do not publish standard prices: we reply within 24 hours with an itemised, line-by-line quote.

Two days are enough when every hour has a reason to exist: a clear direction on the first morning, written decisions on the second evening. The setting, in Paris as in Marrakech, does the rest.
