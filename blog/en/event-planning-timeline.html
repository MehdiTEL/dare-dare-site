Event planning timeline: the free template, from D-90 to D+7

Agence Dare-Dare · October 2026 · Free PDF tool

In short A 38-step timeline, from D-90 to D+7, in five phases: frame, build, refine, lock, close. For each step: when, what, who. Free, as a PDF. Download the free PDF

An event is planned backwards: start from the day itself and work back. This table, which suits a company party as well as a launch, lists the steps in order. Replace D with your event date and tick as you go.

For an event abroad, start earlier, from D-120 to D-180: venues, travel and permits take longer.

What's inside

  2. D-60 to D-30: build it
  3. D-30 to D-7: refine it
  4. D-7 to D: lock it
  5. D+1 to D+7: wrap it up
