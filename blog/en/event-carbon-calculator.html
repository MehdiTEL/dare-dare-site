Indicative, non-certified estimates. This calculator relies on the ADEME Base Carbone and other public sources, with uncertainties of 20 to 70% depending on the line. It does not replace a carbon assessment carried out with a recognised method. Flights include contrail effects.

How to reduce it

  • Train rather than plane whenever possible: on Paris-Lyon the gap is huge.
  • A mostly vegetarian, seasonal, local menu.
  • Hire and reuse decor rather than buying it, and limit single-use goodies.
  • Choose a venue reachable by public transport, and share transfers.

Method and sources

Factors come from the ADEME Base Carbone (open API data.ademe.fr), Agribalyse for meals and Nos Gestes Climat for hotels, accessed on 24 October 2026. A glass of wine counts as 12.5 cl, a beer 25 cl, a coffee 10 cl. Venue energy is an order of magnitude: average consumption of cafés, hotels and restaurants per hour and per m². Decor and goodies entered in euros use ADEME monetary ratios (2023). Lines marked "Dare-Dare estimate" have no official factor.
