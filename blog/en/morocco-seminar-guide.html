Before you start

A seminar in Morocco is won or lost before departure: season, city, venue, flights, paperwork. This guide gathers what we check on every brief, in the order the questions come up. It contains no prices: everything is tailor-made and quoted on request, and a figure published today would be wrong tomorrow. It gives you the budget line items, so nothing is forgotten.

A word on who is writing: in Morocco, Dare-Dare is its own DMC (Destination Management Company), based between Paris and Casablanca. In practice we design and run your seminar ourselves, with a dedicated project manager, local suppliers we know, and a team reachable in French. We cover Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier, Fez and Essaouira. To compare several agencies before you choose: the best agencies for a corporate retreat in Morocco in 2027.
