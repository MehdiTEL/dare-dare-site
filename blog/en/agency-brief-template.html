The brief template: 10 questions to answer before calling an event agency

Agence Dare-Dare · October 2026 · Free PDF tool

In short The 10 questions behind a good brief: why, for whom, when, where, what budget, what guests should remember, the highlight, the constraints, who decides and how to measure success. With an example of a good answer for each. Free, as a PDF to fill in. Download the free PDF

A good brief fits on one page. It saves three weeks of back-and-forth, and it forces you to clarify what you actually want before talking budget and venues. Take 20 minutes, answer every question even briefly, and write "not sure yet" when that is the case: it is a useful answer, and we will turn it into a starting question.

This template works for every format: product launch, brand event, company party for your own employees, seminar, press trip, dinner.

A preview

1. Why are you organizing this event?

Why it matters. An event with no single objective becomes an event that pleases everyone and moves no one. The objective decides the venue, the format, the tone and what you will measure afterwards.

Example of a good answer. "Launch our new fragrance to 80 journalists and content creators, and get at least 30 posts within 7 days." Or: "Thank our 250 employees after a year of reorganization and make them want to start January together."
