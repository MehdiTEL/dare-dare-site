2027 calendar of key dates to time your event (France and Morocco)

Agence Dare-Dare · October 2026 · Free PDF tool

In short The 2027 dates that matter for an event, month by month: bank holidays and long weekends, school holidays, Fashion Weeks, major trade fairs, Cannes Film Festival and Cannes Lions, Ramadan and holidays in Morocco, with the right moments and what to avoid each month. Free, as a PDF. Download the free PDF

France and Morocco, month by month. Last checked: 24 October 2026.

How to use it: find your month, look at what else is happening (public holidays, school breaks, trade fairs, fashion weeks, religious festivals), then choose a date that does not compete with the rest. Each month has "Good time for" and "Avoid for". Every date points to a numbered source [S1], [S2]... listed at the end.

Three reading rules:

  1. Dates marked "estimated" (Ramadan, Eid, Islamic New Year, Mawlid) can move by a day: in Morocco they are set by moon sighting.
  2. Dates marked "to be confirmed" are not published yet. We invent none.
  3. "Our view" marks agency advice, not a sourced fact.
